Monday is the 500th day of the war triggered by Hamas’ Oct. 7, 2023 attack into southern Israel.A tenuous ceasefire in the Gaza Strip has held for nearly a month. But the current phase of the truce is set to expire in early March and it is unclear if the sides will extend it, begin negotiations for a more lasting ceasefire or resume fighting.Here are some numbers that show the scale of death and devastation. Sources include the Israeli government, the Gaza Health Ministry and U.N. agencies.People killed in Israel on Oct. 7, 2023: Around 1,200Hostages taken into Gaza: 251Hostages remaining in Gaza: 73, including 3 taken before Oct. 7, 2023Hostages in Gaza believed to be dead: 36, including one from before Oct. 7, 2023Palestinians killed in Gaza: Over 48,200 (This figure from the Gaza Health Ministry does not distinguish between combatants and civilians, but the ministry says more than half of the dead were women and children)
Palestinians wounded in Gaza: Over 111,600Israeli soldiers killed since Oct. 7, 2023: 846Rockets fired at Israel from Gaza since Oct. 7, 2023: Over 10,000Percentage of Gaza’s population displaced: Around 90%
